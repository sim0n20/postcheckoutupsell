# Privacy policy checklist

Before App Store submission, replace this checklist with your company's legal privacy policy and publish it at a public HTTPS URL.

The app processes merchant shop identifiers, Shopify order identifiers, and customer-supplied personalisation values such as names or gift messages solely to provide personalised post-purchase fulfillment. Define your retention period, subprocessors/hosting provider, security practices, merchant/customer rights process, controller/processor roles, international transfers, and privacy contact. The app implements Shopify's customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact webhook endpoints; shop/redact deletes stored shop data.

This file is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.
